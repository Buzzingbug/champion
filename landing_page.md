## 🔥 Core Capabilities

### 💰 Private Digital Banking (The Economy Engine)
Give your users a reason to grind. Every member gets access to a hyper-secure digital vault where they can hoard custom server currency (Supercoins, VibeCoins, etc.).
- **Live Leaderboards:** Watch your members compete for the top spot.
- **Micro-Transactions:** Deduct currency when users post in premium channels or play minigames.
- **Pay-to-Speak:** Restrict access to elite VIP channels where users *must* maintain a minimum net worth just to type.

### 🎮 High-Stakes Gamification Arenas
Engagement isn't accidental; it's engineered. We've built highly persistent, image-processing arenas where users spend their capital to syndicate content and vote.
- **Smash or Pass / Kiss, Fuck, Marry:** Users submit photos for the community to vote on. Every vote earns the voter coins, and every submission costs coins.
- **Left or Right:** Users seamlessly drag-and-drop two images into a channel; the bot automatically stitches them together into a beautiful side-by-side debate embed.
- **Interactive Puzzles:** Drop an image, and the bot magically scrambles it into a sliding puzzle or jigsaw for the community to solve for a cash bounty!

### 📈 Get Paid to Post
Stop begging people to post in your media channels. Our automated **Category & Channel Rewards** system instantly pays users a set amount of currency every time they upload a photo or video to designated channels. *Incentives drive action.*

### 🏆 Automated Weekly Dividends
Set your engagement on autopilot. Every week, the bot runs a temporal sweep of your database, calculates the most upvoted submissions across all your minigames, and autonomously posts a beautifully formatted "Weekly Winners" dashboard, distributing massive grand prizes to the winners.
